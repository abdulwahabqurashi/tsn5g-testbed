Testbed architecture

TSN time flows from a wired grandmaster, through the AMRC NW-TT (in the UPF), across the 5G radio inside an Ethernet PDU session, to the device-side end station. The radio hop is the only non-deterministic element.

Master offset over time

Time error between the Pi end-station clock and the grandmaster, per second (servo locked). Centred on zero ⇒ no fixed asymmetry; excursions track the radio.

One-way path delay (5G transit)

The ~19 ms is the 5G round-trip ÷ 2. It drifts 16.9–22.7 ms over tens of seconds — this non-stationarity, not a fixed offset, is the dominant error source. E2E PTP assumes a stable, symmetric path; 5G shared-channel scheduling breaks that.

Offset distribution

Most samples cluster low (best 3 µs); the long tail is radio-induced spikes (HARQ / dynamic scheduling). Median |offset| 649 µs, 71% within ±1 ms.

Key findings

  • ValidatedPTP transported successfully over 5G. The end-station locked to the grandmaster (servo s2) across a live 5G Ethernet PDU session, the UPF NW-TT bridging gPTP both directions — uplink Delay_Req and downlink Sync/Announce.
  • CapableSub-100 µs is reachable — best sample 3 µs; tens-of-µs sync occurs whenever radio latency is momentarily stable.
  • MeasuredRMS 1072 µs, median |offset| 649 µs, 42% within ±500 µs, 71% within ±1 ms. Mean bias ≈ 0 ⇒ the error is not a fixed asymmetry and not filterable jitter.
  • Root causeNon-stationary 5G latency. One-way path delay roams 16.9–22.7 ms; offset excursions correlate 1:1 with path-delay steps. The limit is the radio, not PTP or timestamping.
  • ConstraintTransparent-clock correction is structurally bounded on commercial hardware. NW-TT sees only its software residence (~hundreds of ns); the ~19 ms radio transit is outside any translator's window. A true correction needs a DS-TT timestamping vs the 5G reference clock — not exposed by the RM500U-CNV.

Interpretation & roadmap

  • WorksNative L2 + gPTP over 5G, bidirectional, device-side HW timestamping. The 5G system is capable of tens-of-µs sync.
  • CeilingSet by 5G radio-latency variability — not the PTP stack or timestamping.
  • Lever 1Deterministic radio: TSC Assistance Info → gNB scheduling (configured grants) makes path delay stationary — the condition under which this test already hit tens of µs.
  • Lever 2Independent reference: GNSS-PPS at both ends (sub-µs) sidesteps carrying time over the air. RM500U-CNV exposes no GNSS/PPS.
  • Lever 3True 5G transparent clock: DS-TT/NW-TT timestamping vs a common 5G reference (SIB9 referenceTimeInfo) — not exposed to the host by commercial modems today.
